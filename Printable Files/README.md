# HOW TO PRINT!

### Settings That Are Needed!

- Supports -> Enabled (recomended auto tree supports)
- Infill -> 10% or Higher (recomended 15%)
- Walls -> 2 or Higher (recomended 2)
- Use a brim on the parts where less of the part touches the bed itself

### How to arange on the print bed!
- Put the parts/part on the print bed in slicer
- Click Auto Rotate
- Then click Auto Arange
